# Abridge × Anthropic × Lightspeed event facts

Verified: 2026-07-16

Primary public source: [Cerebral Valley event page](https://cerebralvalley.ai/events/~/e/abridge-hackathon)

## Confirmed

- Event: **The Future of Agentic AI in Healthcare — Abridge × Anthropic × Lightspeed**.
- Date and time: Saturday, July 18, 2026, 09:00–22:00 PDT.
- Public location: San Francisco, California. An [organizer's public announcement](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/cerebralvalley_healthcare-is-one-of-the-hardest-spaces-to-activity-7476024751556313088-KUwf) names SHACK15; the approved registration remains the authority for the street address and arrival instructions.
- Format: fully in person.
- Maximum team size: two.
- Prompt: **Build Agents for Healthcare Clinics**.
- Scope: select one clinical or operational workflow and make it faster, smarter, or safer with an agentic system.
- Delivery expectation: ship something a clinician or patient-facing team could plausibly use on Monday.
- Event resources: Abridge resources including clinician feedback; cross-domain participants; access to leaders and engineers from Abridge, Anthropic, and Lightspeed.
- The event framing asks for real-world clinical impact while acknowledging messy data, high stakes, and near-zero margin for error. This is a problem statement, not permission to claim that an unvalidated prototype saves lives.

## Recovered application-waiver terms

The earlier application payload states that entrants retain ownership while granting organizers and partners broad perpetual non-exclusive rights, and that material shared at the event is non-confidential. Confirm the operative terms in the approved registration before exposing proprietary methods or restricted data.
