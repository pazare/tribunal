Solution · mechanism · 1 of 3

Consensus is earned, not generated — five instruments, each grounded in a measured failure mode.

Sealed blind ballots, rotated candidate order
Every material round is cryptographically committed before reveal; per-recipient order permutation controls position bias.
⇠ anchoring & majority exposure — the attack surface of multi-turn influence
Evidence-change certificates
No vote change without naming the new fact, corrected inference, or normative trade-off. Uncertified changes flag as possible capitulation.
⇠ misleading suggestions universally effective across 9 frontier models — arXiv:2602.13093
Capitulation detector — latent vs. behavioral 2×2
Public reasoning graph compared against the emitted vote; unchanged warrant + flipped vote = unfaithful capitulation, not convergence.
⇠ ~50% of think-mode flips leave a correct trace — arXiv:2605.29087
No self-reported confidence in ratification
Calibration comes from outcome-anchored evaluation only; refusals and timeouts are ledgered non-votes, never fabricated ballots.
⇠ confidence-aware defenses (CARG) fail on reasoning models — reasoning-induced overconfidence
Sen maximal-set abstention
When no admissible aggregation separates the maximal set, the case is declared underdetermined → escalation to a credentialed human is a first-class outcome.
⇠ premature closure is the dominant 21-model failure; forced consensus reproduces it

Two-stage Delphi

Stage A — Epistemic board. Settles what is known: timeline, contradictions, must-not-miss differential, evidence map with minority report. No treatment talk allowed.

Stage B — Action board. Weighs what is safe and feasible under a lexicographic order: safety and patient rights are admission criteria; cost only compares admissible options.

Unit of commitment: the clinical commitment span — the smallest statement binding the system to a fact, action, urgency, or escalation — each carrying evidence, dissent, and expiry in its envelope.

Telemetry: reasoning graphs are exported DRL-compatible (clinically weighted graph-edit discrepancies — arXiv:2602.09945), so the system is measurable by construction.

Business design

  • Enterprise platform fee — $300K–$1.5M/yr by institution size and integration depth
  • Bounded per-case usage fee — deliberation, retrieval, audit storage
  • Specialist pass-through — fair-market-value professional fees; transparent administration margin where permissible
  • Year-one design-partner program — $3–7M total, staged to the evaluation ladder

Structurally excluded

Treatment-linked commissions · referral bounties · facility-steering revenue · payer savings bonuses before safety and equity validation. Alignment with AKS/Stark is a design constraint, not an afterthought.

  • Sealed ballots, anonymized cross-exam, rotated candidate order
  • Binding safety veto under a named rule; minority reports preserved
  • Hash-chained ledger; anyone can re-verify (POST /api/verify)
  • A1–A12 auditability scorecard with anti-spoof guards
  • Fail-closed timeouts — no fabricated ballots
